Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Solutions ClearShield ("ClearShield", "we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this site and through our services, why we collect it, how we protect it and what your rights are, under Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (as amended by Law 25) and applicable Canadian law.

1. Person in charge of personal information

The owner of Solutions ClearShield is the person in charge of the protection of personal information. For any question, request or complaint: info@solutionsclearshield.com · 514-775-9418 · Montreal, Quebec.

2. Information we collect

  • Contact form: name, email, phone and project details.
  • Assessment and services: project address, window measurements and photos, glass type and other information needed for the quote and the work.
  • Communications: your exchanges with us by phone, email or text message.
  • Technical data: IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and where the visit came from (for example an ad), collected through cookies according to your choices (section 5).

3. Why we use it

  • To answer your request and call you back.
  • To plan the on-site assessment, prepare the quote and carry out the work.
  • To provide follow-up, after-sales service and warranty handling.
  • To measure our advertising and improve the site, only with your consent.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We only collect the information needed for these purposes. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

4. Consent and communications

By filling in the form or contacting us, you consent to our using your information to answer your request, including by reaching you by phone, email or text message. You can withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us. Every promotional message includes an easy way to unsubscribe, in line with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation.

5. Cookies and advertising

This site uses:

  • essential cookies needed for the site to work, for example to remember your language and your cookie choice;
  • only with your consent, measurement and advertising tools such as Google Analytics, the Google Ads tag and the Meta Pixel (Facebook and Instagram). They measure our ads and show you relevant advertising.

Measurement and advertising tools stay off until you click "Accept". You can change your choice at any time with the "Manage cookies" link at the bottom of the site, and manage your ad preferences in your browser and in your Google and Meta accounts.

6. Sharing with third parties

We only share your information with providers that help us run the business, and only as needed: our customer management platform (HighLevel), our web host (Cloudflare, which also stores photos attached to quote requests), our email, text message and phone tools, and Google and Meta for ad measurement if you consented. These providers must protect your information.

Some providers store information outside Quebec, including in the United States. Before any such transfer, we assess whether the information will be adequately protected, as the law requires. We may also disclose information when required by law.

7. Retention and security

We keep your information for as long as needed for the purposes above and to meet our legal and warranty obligations, then destroy or anonymize it. We take reasonable measures to protect it against loss, theft and unauthorized access: restricted access, protected accounts and reputable providers. If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious injury, we will notify you and the Commission d'accès à l'information.

8. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • access your information and get a copy of it;
  • have it corrected if it is inaccurate, incomplete or ambiguous;
  • withdraw your consent to its use;
  • receive your information in a structured, commonly used technological format;
  • ask that it stop being disseminated or be de-indexed, where the law allows.

Email us at info@solutionsclearshield.com. We will reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can file a complaint with Quebec's Commission d'accès à l'information (cai.gouv.qc.ca).

9. Minors

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information about children under 14.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of the page shows the version in effect.
